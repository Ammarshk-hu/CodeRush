## 🔷 SECTION 10 — RULES AND GUIDELINES

- **Heading:** `RULES AND GUIDELINES`

1. Maintain professional and respectful behavior toward all participants and organizers.
2. Use of offensive, harmful, or inappropriate content is strictly prohibited.
3. All submissions must be **original work created during the event duration**.
4. Plagiarism or copying complete external projects leads to **immediate disqualification**.
5. Only registered members can be part of a team.
6. Collaboration is allowed only within the same registered team.
7. Teams with incomplete or inaccessible submissions may be disqualified.
8. AI-generated content must be disclosed and should not form the entirety of the submission.
9. All team members must be present during the demo/presentation round.

---

**Q1: Who can participate in CodeRush 1.0?**
> Any college student from any branch or year across India. Both individuals and teams of up to 4 members are welcome.

**Q2: What is the team size?**
> Teams can have 1 to 4 members. Solo participation is also allowed.

**Q3: Is there a registration fee?**
> *(Fee details to be updated — check the Payment section or registration portal.)*

**Q4: Do I need prior coding experience?**
> Not necessarily. CodeRush includes Ideathon and UI/UX tracks where coding is minimal. All skill levels are welcome.

**Q5: Is the event online or offline?**
> *(To be confirmed — online/offline/hybrid. Check official announcements.)*

**Q6: What do I need to submit at the end?**
> A working prototype, Figma design, or idea presentation deck along with a GitHub link (if applicable). See the Submission Protocol section for full details.

**Q7: Can I use pre-built templates or open-source code?**
> You can use libraries and frameworks, but the core solution must be created during the event. Submitting full pre-built templates as original work is not allowed.

**Q8: Will mentors be available during the event?**
> Yes, mentors will be available during the hackathon for guidance, reviews, and feedback sessions.

**Q9: How will projects be judged?**
> Projects will be evaluated on:
> - **Innovation & Creativity** — Is the idea original and bold?
> - **Design Quality** — Is the UI/UX intuitive and polished?
> - **Feasibility** — Can this solution actually be built/implemented?
> - **Presentation** — Is the pitch clear and compelling?
> - **Impact** — Does the solution solve a real problem?

**Q10: Where do I register?**
> Registration is available on the official portal. Click the `Register` button in the navigation bar or visit the Unstop link.

**Q11: What if my team member drops out mid-event?**
> Remaining members can still continue. Notify the organizers immediately in such cases.

**Q12: Will certificates be provided?**
> Yes! All participants will receive a **digital participation certificate**. Winners and runners-up will receive special certificates and prizes.

## 🎨 DESIGN REFERENCE NOTES (Cyberpunk / Dark Terminal Theme)

### Color Palette

| Role | Color |
|------|-------|
| Background | Deep Black (`#000000` / `#0a0a0a`) |
| Primary Accent | Cyan (`#00e5ff` / `cyan-400`) |
| Secondary Accent | Red (`#ef4444` / `red-500`) |
| Text Primary | White (`#ffffff`) |
| Text Muted | Gray (`#9ca3af` / `gray-400`) |
| Border / Glow | Cyan with opacity (`cyan-500/30`) |
| Card Background | Dark glass (`#111827` / semi-transparent) |

### Typography

- **Font Family:** Monospace / Tech font → `Space Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, or `Courier New`
- **Headings:** ALL-CAPS, letter-spaced, bold
- **Body Text:** Light weight, muted gray, readable line-height

### Visual Design Style

- **Theme:** Cyberpunk / Dark Hacker / Terminal aesthetic
- **Background:** Near-black with subtle dot/grid pattern overlay
- **Borders:** Glowing cyan and red neon edges on cards
- **Animations:**
  - Typewriter text effect on hero heading
  - Orbit / rotating ring animations in About section
  - Pulse/glow effect on stats and badges
  - Scan-line overlay for the terminal feel
- **Badges:** Small pill-shaped status tags → `System Online • Secure Connection`
- **Cards:** Dark glass-morphism with glowing left border or edges
- **Section Labels:** Small all-caps tag above each section heading (alternating cyan / red)
- **CTA Buttons:** Solid accent background (cyan) for primary · outlined/ghost for secondary
